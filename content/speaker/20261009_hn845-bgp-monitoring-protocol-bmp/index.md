I joined [Ethan Banks](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ethanbanks/) and [Drew Conry-Murray](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andrewconrymurray/) on [Heavy Networking](https://packetpushers.net/podcast/heavy-networking/) to talk about the BGP Monitoring Protocol (BMP): how service providers use it in real life, and what the architecture behind it looks like.

We covered how I collect the data, why capturing routes both pre- and post-policy matters, how to deal with an overwhelming number of prefixes, what I'm planning next, and how you can get started with BMP yourself.
