faq:
  - q: "Who are these courses for?"
    a: "Network engineers who want to become automation engineers. Each course has a level (Novice, Intermediate, Advanced) so you know where to start."
  - q: "Do I need to know Python?"
    a: "Not for the Novice courses. We start from zero, in a network context. Intermediate courses assume you can write and run a simple Python script."
  - q: "What do I need to bring?"
    a: "A laptop with admin rights, so you can install VScode and Docker. You get setup instructions a week before the course. The lab runs on your own laptop, so you keep it afterwards."
  - q: "Do I need network equipment?"
    a: "No. We use virtual network devices (Containerlab) and simulated devices, so everything runs on your laptop."
  - q: "How big are the groups?"
    a: "Small. The group size depends on the course and is listed on each course page, but it's never more than 10 participants. That leaves room for your questions and your own use cases."
  - q: "What language is the course in?"
    a: "Dutch or English, depending on the group. The material is in English."
  - q: "Can you run a course for our team?"
    a: "Yes. In-company courses are adapted to your environment and skills. <a href=\"/#contact\">Get in touch</a>."
  - q: "Are the courses online or in person?"
    a: "Public courses are in person, in the Netherlands. In-company courses can be on site or remote."
  - q: "How do I pay, and do I get an invoice?"
    a: "You book and pay through the sign-up link of each date. You get an invoice with VAT, ready for your employer."
